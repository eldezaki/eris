Profile

Eris is one of the main heroines of Mushoku Tensei: Jobless Reincarnation, the light novel by Rifujin na Magonote (illustrated by Shirotaka). She first appears as the spoiled, violent granddaughter of a powerful lord, and grows into one of the strongest swordswomen in the story. This page is an unofficial fan archive. Details from later arcs are kept light, and the one real spoiler is folded away under Small details.

The Boreas house

Eris grows up inside the Boreas Greyrat family, a noble house of the Asura Kingdom with a reputation for martial pride. Her grandfather Sauros Boreas Greyrat sits at the top of it, and her father Philip Boreas Greyrat runs the household. Her mother is Hilda.

It is a house that expects obedience, and Eris has very little of it. The early chapters make the point by having her chase off tutor after tutor until Philip hires a ten-year-old from a branch family. That child is Rudeus, and his arrival is what sets the rest of her life in motion.

“Boreas” is also the Greek name for the north wind. The series does not make anything of it, but it suits someone this cold-blooded in a fight and this hot-blooded everywhere else.

Temper

Eris is the series’ clearest tsundere: loud, proud and quick to use her fists, with a protective streak she would sooner die than admit to. At the start she hits first and asks nothing. Fans gave her the “Mad Dog” name for the way she charges at monsters and at anyone who sneers at Rudeus.

What drives her

Pride is the engine. She hates losing, hates being talked down to, and hates being protected more than almost anything. Her loyalty is the fuel. She is merciless toward people who wish Rudeus harm, and she will train herself past exhaustion rather than be the one who needs saving.

What changes

Her growth is not about losing the fire. She learns to read, to count, to speak politely when it matters, and to listen. By the time she wears the Greyrat name alone, the temper is still there. It just has a direction.

Her story

A short route through her arc, kept spoiler-light.

  1. The unwilling studentPhilip hires Rudeus to tutor her. She rejects him by force, then slowly accepts reading, math and basic magic. Ghislaine Dedoldia is hired for the sword.
  2. The teleportation incidentA teleportation event throws Eris and Rudeus far from home, onto the Demon Continent. She has to survive in a place no noble upbringing prepared her for.
  3. Dead EndWith Ruijerd of the Superd tribe, they form the adventurer party Dead End. Real monsters replace practice dummies and her swordsmanship hardens quickly.
  4. The burdenOn the long road back toward Fittoa she begins to feel like a weight on the person she wants to stand beside. That feeling drives the hardest decision of her early life.
  5. A new nameAt fifteen she sets aside the Boreas name and becomes Eris Greyrat, choosing the family she picked over the one she was born into.
  6. The sword kingHer later training is what earns the Sword King and North Saint titles. Her story continues through Season 2 and the 2026 Season 3 of the anime.

The sword

Eris begins with a wooden sword and an instinct for violence. Ghislaine, a beastkin swordswoman, gives that instinct a form. Eris studies the Sword God Style and also earns rank in the North God Style, which suits a fighter who prefers to take the fight to the other side.

Her North God Style rank is Saint. In the Adventurers’ Guild she is rated A.

How she fights

She is not a patient duelist. Her fights are fast, forward and loud. The difference between early and later Eris is control: the same aggression, but with footwork and timing behind it. Magic was never her gift, and she compensates with the sword until the blade is enough on its own.

Bonds

Rudeus Greyrat

Tutor, student, party-mate and the person her story is built around. They begin as a lonely noble and a hired tutor and end up as each other’s anchor. The relationship is the center of her character, and she shapes his arc as much as he shapes hers.

Ghislaine Dedoldia

Her sword teacher, and the first adult to treat her anger as something worth training rather than punishing.

Ruijerd Superdia

The Superd warrior who guards the pair across the Demon Continent. Under him, Eris learns how a veteran stays alive.

Philip, Hilda and Sauros

The family she leaves behind and keeps coming back to. They are the reason she starts out the way she does, and the reason her choice of the Greyrat name carries weight.

Small details

The hairband

Across her many outfits, including the cover art of Volume 22, Eris never takes the hairband off. It is her one constant.

The wooden sword

Her first weapon is a practice sword, and it appears in the character’s official imagery. It is a small reminder that she starts as a child and earns every rank.

Spoiler: how the web novel ends for her

In the web novel she dies of old age at 74, after collapsing during a training session. The anime and manga have not reached that point.
